NPN SCM Hackathon — E2 + P2
SmartSupply Command Center
Closing the gap between planning and execution. Real-time truck tracking, automated yard management, and integrated S&OP — unified in one platform.
The Problem
Two critical gaps are slowing supply chain operations every single day.
E2
Where's My Truck?
Warehouse managers are left in the dark about truck locations, estimated arrival times, and dock door assignments — leading to reactive decisions and constant uncertainty. Manual scheduling wastes 20+ hours per week.
P2
Planning in a Vacuum
Demand forecasts are created without live execution constraints. Seasonal spikes cause chaos, manual replanning takes days, and there is no visibility between what was planned and what is actually happening on the ground.
Our Solution
SmartSupply Command Center — bidirectional integration between planning and execution.
🚛
E2 Execution Module
Real-time truck scheduling, automated yard allocation, and live operational visibility across all facilities and fleets.
📊
P2 Planning Module
Demand forecasting fully integrated with execution constraints — dock capacity, truck availability, and yard space baked into every single plan.
⚡
Bidirectional Integration
Execution data automatically reshapes plans in real-time. When a dock goes offline or a truck is delayed, the plan knows instantly.
